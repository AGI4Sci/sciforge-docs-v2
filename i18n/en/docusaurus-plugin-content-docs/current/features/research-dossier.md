# Research Dossier

The research dossier puts the research summary, sources, key artifacts, versions, and limitations together, for review and delivery. After opening a research record, read the results and the limitations that need attention first, then look at earlier versions as needed. Process records come from [Research Checkpoints](./research-checkpoints.md), and evidence relationships are in [Evidence DAG](./evidence-dag.md).

## Opening it

- Entry: **Research** in the right toolbar (expand **More tools** if it is not shown); the right panel is titled **Research** as well; you can also click **Open research dossier** on a session result card.
- To see history, open a specific artifact version or research record, and first confirm which record you are reading.
- Prerequisite: the session is bound to a workspace; after research checkpoints or artifact versions have been saved, you can view the matching detailed records.

![SciForge research dossier panel](/img/docs/guides/research-dossier.jpg)
*The research dossier brings the research narrative, version position, credibility summary, and the limitations to consider onto one reading surface.*

## Read and check a record

1. Finish a round of research, and click **Open research dossier** on the result card.
2. Confirm the selected research record and version, and read the overview, research sources, declared files, and key artifacts.
3. Look at **Limitations to consider** and **Provenance breakpoints**, and check item by item the missing sources, unverified results, and reproduction conditions.
4. To review history, load earlier versions in the **Versions** area, and use **Compare exact version** to check the differences.
5. When you need an earlier result, choose **Restore as new version** and confirm, then check the restored result and the actual files. The existing history is kept.

When the selected record is missing or fails verification, the dossier shows a prompt. Check the record and the related files, and use it for delivery only after the problem is resolved.

## Preview and delivery

Use **Open verified preview** to view the selected content, or **Export Bundle V2** to package it for delivery. Exporting writes workspace files and asks for confirmation; check that all the required related versions and files are present and allowed to be accessed and exported, and state what has not yet been verified. Before sharing, also check the bundle for sensitive data.

Restoring and importing an old session need confirmation as prompted. An imported old session stays marked as having an incomplete history, with the missing provenance information flagged. You can also turn automatic recording on or off here; for its behavior, see [Research Checkpoints](./research-checkpoints.md).

## Tips

- What is shown depends on the checkpoints, artifact versions, and evidence the task has saved. If there are no records yet, finish and save a round of the task first.
- When part of the record cannot be read for the moment, the matching content may be unavailable, while the rest of the verified content can still be read.
- Empty blocks and inapplicable compute, evidence, or review blocks are omitted. Open Evidence DAG when you need the full evidence relationships.
- The summaries and references in the dossier need to be checked against the actual files, especially after the related files were modified, moved, or deleted.
